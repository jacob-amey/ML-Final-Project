Your saved models

The earlier pilots have different label spaces and splits. Create ML has training exposure to much of the new test set. The final CNN, frozen Compact177 full-data reference and simple rule share the reported frozen 177-class test protocol. Compact177 is separate from the three-class pilots. The app does not train models or rerun held-out test evaluation.

GETTING STARTED

A local app, start to finish.

  1. Start LeafCheck

    Double-click Launch LeafCheck.command in the app folder. It opens LeafCheck in your usual browser. Leave the terminal window open.

  2. Pick a leaf photo

    Choose a JPEG or PNG photo, drag one onto the upload area, or select an example.

  3. Run the comparison

    All four saved models process the photo on your Mac. Read their predictions and scores, or export the results as a JSON file.

  4. Stop when you’re finished

    Go to the launcher’s terminal window and press Control-C. Restart by double-clicking the launcher again.

No account or internet connection is needed during use. If the connection is lost, restart the launcher and choose “Retry connection.”
