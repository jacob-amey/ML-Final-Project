# Follow-up rice healthy and brown spot source

The [RiceLeaf-06-2777 release](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/4sdw6gstgs/1) lists six rice conditions from Monirampur, Jashore. Healthy and brown spot are directly compatible with two frozen LeafCheck labels that performed poorly in the prior field check. The other disease folders were outside the targeted intake. The author-declared reuse license is CC BY 4.0.

Two complete archives passed byte count and SHA-256 checks and contain the expected 267 healthy and 517 brown-spot JPEG entries. A fixed sample of twelve per label decodes as original JPEGs. The master and PlantDoc exact-byte, decoded-pixel and visual-hash checks flagged no matches in this sample. The contact sheet shows close-up leaves, with a mixture of outdoor and plain backgrounds. Several views appear related. Those checks establish limited intake evidence, not independently observed plants.

The header census found EXIF dates on 550 of 784 files. The 179 dated healthy files all came from March 4, 2026. None of the 371 dated brown-spot files has that date. Brown spot spans eleven dated groups, with February 27 the largest (143 files). The remaining 234 files lack a recorded date. The known dates separate class labels in this source. Date-based internal validation would risk comparing acquisition conditions instead of disease, and the original leaf identities remain unavailable. Keep this source unscored and unassigned. Independent healthy and brown-spot photos with both labels represented in several capture groups would better support a broad confirmation.

Restore the two original ZIPs under `data/raw/leafcheck_field_v12/rice-2026/` using the publisher URLs in the [archive manifest](../../data/manifests/leafcheck_rice_2026_intake.json). Then run `.venv/bin/python scripts/inspect_rice_2026.py` to check archive integrity, reproduce the capture-day census and screen the same original sample.
