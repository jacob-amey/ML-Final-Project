# Cabbage, broccoli and cauliflower intake

The frozen CNN correctly classified zero of nine master validation photos labeled cabbage Alternaria leaf spot. Broccoli and cauliflower Alternaria also have small original training pools of 32 and 22 photos. This intake screened additional sources for an independently testable improvement.

- The [Ayawli cabbage collection](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/sjmgzhwrxv/1) separates 205 nominal originals from augmented archives. The audit used its combined original package and skipped all augmentation packages, including a second listing for downy mildew. It flagged 21 possible master overlaps, including 16 of 70 Alternaria files. Two originals had unsupported image encoding in the intake and two fell into conflicting-label clusters. No candidate provided an EXIF capture time. Contact sheets showed varied image styles, laboratory-like backgrounds and web-image markings. The surviving 52 Alternaria files need independent source and label evidence before they can support a held-out comparison.
- The [Ikteshad vegetable leaf collection](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/rh8c5tgg5k/1) labels the images as processed. The four inspected archives contain 460 photos. All 107 cabbage Alternaria files fall in two device and capture-date blocks, lasting six and eleven minutes by EXIF time. Of 219 broccoli Alternaria files, 208 share one date. All 56 cauliflower Alternaria files share a single capture minute and 42 were flagged as repeated views. The cabbage contact sheet also shows closely related views despite many passing automatic screening. The filename sequence cannot identify independent specimens.

Both publishers declare CC BY 4.0. All five file hashes matched the publisher catalog. The 665-row screen checked raw bytes, rotated/reflected decoded-pixel hashes and perceptual hashes against the frozen master and previously registered field datasets. Automatic clearance alone does not resolve crop edits, different views of the same leaf, or original source attribution. Assign no training or evaluation roles in either collection. A credible study needs a separately documented field source or independently labeled specimens and should preserve the acquisition blocks intact.
